About the owner

Hello my name is John O'Reilly and I have been working on cars for about 25 years now.
My interest was first sparked when I would see my father working on a old mustang when I was still 6 years old.


Why I started Wheels on Fire

I started this business after my late father passed away as we both shared a deep love for fixing our friends and families cars and always mentioned the dream of opening a shop. So now I have taken up the mantle to do what we have always dreamed of and I hope that my business allows everyone to have the car that they have always wanted.
